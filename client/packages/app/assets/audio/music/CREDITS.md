# Music while alone in a call

Three tracks are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 by their authors on OpenGameArt.
No attribution is required; it is given here anyway.
The electronic track was made for slim-m and is rebuilt from `assets/audio/lounge/generate.py`.
Each was loudness-normalised to -20 LUFS, given a short fade at each end so the loop point is soft, and re-encoded as 96 kbps MP3 from the source files linked below.

| File | Style | Title | Author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lofi.mp3 | Lofi | Chill lofi inspired | omfgdude | https://opengameart.org/content/chill-lofi-inspired |
| jazz.mp3 | Jazz | Elevator Music | Alex McCulloch (Pro Sensory) | https://opengameart.org/content/elevator-music |
| electronic.mp3 | Electronic | Quiet Pulse | slim-m | `assets/audio/lounge/generate.py` (renders `wav/electronic.wav`, encoded as 96 kbps MP3) |
| ambient.mp3 | Ambient | Contemplation | Joth | https://opengameart.org/content/contemplation-0 |
