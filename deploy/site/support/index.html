slim-m support

It's a hobby project run by one person, so replies can be slow.

If something's broken

Open an issue at github.com/Slim-m-org/slim-m/issues. Say what you did and what happened, which phone and app version you're on (it's in Settings under About), and a screenshot helps if there's nothing private in it.

Joining a space

You need two things from whoever runs the space: its address and an invite code. On the sign-in screen you put the address in the Server field, then register with the code. The first person to log in on a brand new server becomes its admin.

Forgot your password

There's no email recovery. An admin of your space can give you a one-time reset code, and you use that on the sign-in screen.

Deleting your account

Settings, then Account & devices, then Delete account. It purges your personal data and signs out every device, and your old messages stay without your name on them. There's more on this in the privacy policy.

Reporting a message or a person

Hold a message and choose Report message, or open someone's profile and choose Report user. Reports go to the admins of the space you're in, and you can block someone from the same menu. Admins can delete messages, time people out, and remove members.

If something on the official server isn't being handled, or it's something illegal, open an issue without posting the content itself and I'll look at it.

Running your own server

The walkthrough is deploy/README.md in the repo. You pretty much spin it up in docker, then on your phone you download the app and the first person to log in becomes admin.
